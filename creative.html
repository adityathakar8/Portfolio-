ACADEMIC PROJECTS

Grad school gave me a chance to put a wide range of tools to work: AI, REST APIs, FastAPI, LLMs, you name it. The projects below are a couple of favorites, where I got to dig into telemetry analysis, root cause analysis, and building visualizations that actually help someone make a decision.

VelocityIQ

Predictive Supply Chain & RFM Analysis

Built a predictive supply chain model in Python to analyze over 1 million rows of retail data, optimizing key inventory KPIs like Average Order Value and return rates. The model helped leadership reallocate $50k in capital away from stagnant inventory and into high-growth categories. I also layered in RFM segmentation to prioritize stock for high-value customer clusters, which cut potential revenue loss by 18% and kept availability above 95% for top-tier segments during seasonal surges.

Python Predictive Modeling RFM Segmentation

Project Aero-Mech

Telemetry Analysis on Athlete Performance

Ran a root cause analysis on more than 16,000 rows of telemetry data pulled from wearable sensors to figure out what actually drives athlete performance failures. From there I built a diagnostic and prescriptive model in Python that could separate mechanical issues from cardiovascular fatigue. I brought the biometric data into interactive Tableau dashboards, which pointed to sleep and CNS fatigue as key risk factors, helping catch future performance shutdowns before they happened.
